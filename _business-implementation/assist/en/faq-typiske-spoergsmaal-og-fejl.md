# Assist – FAQ
_Typical questions, errors and answers · v1.0 · 11.06.2026_

Quick answers to the most common questions and errors in Assist. Find your question below. More detailed steps are in the **Assist – employee guide** and the **Assist – super-user guide**.


## For employees (use during the meeting)


**What is the Assist tab called in Salesforce?**

It may be called Assist, Assist Lite, Transcribe or a name your organisation has chosen itself. It sits on the meeting itself (Event).


**The Assist tab is missing on the meeting — what do I do?**

Check that you have an Assist licence/access, and that you have opened a meeting (Event). If it is still missing, contact your super-user (the component needs to be placed on the Event page).


**The microphone doesn't work / there is no transcription.**

Give the browser permission to use the microphone (Chrome or Edge), and select the right microphone under Microphone settings. Also check your internet connection, and start the meeting again. If it continues, contact support.


**The “Start meeting” button is greyed out — why?**

It stays grey until participants have loaded and a microphone has been selected (typically a few seconds). If it stays grey, select a microphone manually and refresh the page.


**What is the button that ends the meeting called?**

“Finish & generate summary”. When you click it, Assist automatically generates the summary.


**I can't see the transcription or the talk-time distribution during the meeting.**

Scroll within the panel — the transcription and talk time are shown there. Points of attention and agreements are only filled in after a couple of minutes (you will see, for example, “X messages until the next update”). This is not an error.


**The summary says “No agreements made yet”.**

This is normal — Assist simply found no specific agreements in the conversation.


**How long does it take to generate the summary?**

Typically under a minute after you click “Finish & generate summary”.


**Where do I find the summary?**

In Salesforce — either on the meeting itself (Event) or in a dedicated tab (several organisations call it, for example, “Summary”). If in doubt, ask your super-user.


**What is the difference between “Live meeting” and “Upload transcription”?**

Live meeting records and transcribes the meeting live. Upload transcription is only for uploading an already existing recording/text.


**I can't find my summary.**

The summary is saved in your CRM once it has been generated — check the meeting (Event) or the “Summary” tab in Salesforce (see “Where do I find the summary”). The transcription is only kept for up to 48 hours; if the summary has not ended up in the CRM, contact support within 48 hours. Remember: the summary is only generated if you have clicked “Finish & generate summary” — if you don't, the transcription is deleted after 48 hours and there is no summary.


**The connection dropped, or the browser closed in the middle of the meeting.**

For brief interruptions the session can be resumed if you return quickly. For longer interruptions the recording may be lost — finish and generate a summary of what has been recorded, or start the meeting over. If in doubt, contact support.


**The transcription is in the wrong language.**

The language is chosen before you start (under settings/the cog). If the transcription is in the wrong language, end the meeting and start over with the right language selected.


**Participants don't load, or a role is wrong.**

Check the participants and roles (customer/employee) on the preparation screen before you start — these are what name the transcription. Wait a moment if they are still loading, and correct the role before starting. If they keep failing to load, refresh the page or contact support.


**Does Assist work in Safari or Firefox?**

Assist is supported in Chrome and Edge. In Safari or Firefox the recording may fail (sometimes without a clear message) — switch to Chrome or Edge.


**What is “Upload transcription”, and can I use it?**

It is an option to upload an already existing transcription instead of recording live — for example a Teams transcription (.vtt file), so that Assist generates a summary from it. If it is not enabled for you, it may not appear — contact &money.


**What are “Meeting goals”?**

Goals that Assist follows and assesses during the meeting. The feature is coming soon and is not yet open to customers.


**How do I get the best recording at an in-person meeting?**

Use an external microphone or headset (not just the built-in laptop mic), place the microphone in the middle of the table, keep it clear of obstructions, and choose a quiet room. Test the microphone before the meeting.


**May I close the laptop when the meeting starts?**

No — leave the laptop open throughout the meeting. If you close it as the meeting begins, the recording may be interrupted.


**Can I switch to other tabs or programs during the recording?**

Yes. You can safely switch between tabs and programs — for example the Present/meeting-presentation tab, PowerPoint, online banking (I*Net) or other tabs — and return without losing the recording.


**Do I need to remember to end the meeting?**

Yes. Always click “Finish & generate summary” so the summary is generated. (Automatic generation, if you forget, is on the way.)


**How do I hold a hybrid meeting (both in-person and online participants)?**

Hybrid meetings — where there are both in-person and online participants — are held in Teams.


**How do I get the summary into an online-banking message?**

Depending on your setup, you can either copy the summary directly from the summary tab into an online-banking message, or locate the summary via a short process. Ask your super-user if in doubt.


## For super-users (Management UI)


**Do I need to choose an Assist version or variant?**

No. &money sets up the Assist version and the template for you.


**Can I create meeting goals?**

Not yet — meeting goals have been developed but are not yet open to customers (“coming soon”). The preview in the guide shows what the feature will be able to do.


**Why are the reporting figures at 0?**

Because meeting goals are not open yet. “Meetings with goals”, “Goal count” and “Goal completion rate” stay at 0 until meeting goals are opened — “Assist meetings” is the primary figure today.


**What do Done / Addressed / Not started mean?**

Done = the goal has been reached. Addressed = touched on along the way, but not reached. Not started = not touched on.


**How often should I check the reporting?**

Monthly, for example. If you see markedly low usage, get in touch with &money.


**How do I confirm that a user has an Assist licence/access?**

Licences and access are assigned by &money / your Entra administrator. If a user can't see Assist, confirm with your Entra admin or &money that the user has access, and that the Assist component has been placed on the Event page.


**Where is it controlled whether the summary lands on the meeting or in a tab?**

This is configured per organisation (not in the Assist screen). You do not set it up as a super-user — confirm your setup with &money if in doubt.


## Data, consent and access


**Does the employee need to inform the customer about the recording?**

Yes. Say, for example: “For this meeting I'm using an AI assistant that records and produces a text of the conversation so I can write a summary — is that all right with you?” Assist's own AI note does not replace your own information.


**How long is the recording/transcription kept?**

The transcription is kept for up to 48 hours and is then deleted automatically. The summary in your CRM is the permanent copy — you don't need to do anything to delete the transcription.


**Is our meeting data used to train AI models?**

No. The processing (transcription and AI) takes place in the EU (Microsoft Azure), and the content is not used for model training.


**The Assist tab hangs on a spinner / doesn't load.**

Click refresh (reload the page), and check that you are logged in to Microsoft 365 (use Chrome or Edge). If the spinner is still there, submit a case with the meeting ID to support — there is a known issue with login in the embedded component that &money is working on.
