# Schedule – FAQ: Getting started
_Quick answers for the new super-user · v1.0 · 12.06.2026_

Quick answers to help you get started with Schedule. The overview and recommended order are in **Schedule – getting started**.


## Getting started


**Where do I start?**

Start in **Meeting setup** (the rules), then **Employees** (make them bookable), then **Service groups** (pools), then **Portals** (the customer-facing page). Follow up with **Reporting** and **Insights**.


**Which guide should I use for what?**

See the ‘Schedule areas’ table in the getting-started guide — each area has its own super-user guide + FAQ.


**What is the difference between Reporting and Insights?**

**Reporting** shows the **actual** meetings booked, looking backwards. **Insights** is a **forecast** of future availability, provided that data is delivered nightly via Salesforce CRM analytics.


**What are the four booking flows?**

**Internal** (case handling), **employee-booked**, **customer-booked** and **portal**. Most importantly: internal skips most rules; the three customer-facing flows share the same rule engine.


**The customer can't book — where do I look?**

It's a **customer-facing** rule: check the **meeting configuration** (is there one for the customer type/topic?), **buffers**/**max. hours per day**, and whether the employee has the right **competencies** and is bookable. See the FAQs for Meeting setup and Employees.


**Who can do what (roles)?**

**Manager** (e.g. service groups/reporting), **Configurator** (meeting setup, employees, portals, etc.), **Admin** (everything, including Playbooks/logs).
