# Playbooks

Playbooks are automation workflows that orchestrate business processes by connecting blocks through a visual editor. They enable automated data processing, integration with AI capabilities, and CRM interactions — all without writing code.

## Quick Start

1. **New to playbooks?** Read the [Introduction]({{ site.baseurl }}/schedule/playbooks/introduction-to-playbooks/) to understand the building blocks
2. **Ready to build?** Follow the [User Guide]({{ site.baseurl }}/schedule/playbooks/playbooks-user-guide/) step by step
3. **Need integration details?** Check the [Integrations]({{ site.baseurl }}/schedule/playbooks/playbooks-integration-guide/) page
4. **See a "Managed" badge on a playbook?** [Managed Playbooks]({{ site.baseurl }}/schedule/playbooks/managed-playbooks/) explains what those are and how forking works
